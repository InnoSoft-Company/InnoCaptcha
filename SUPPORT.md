# Support

Thank you for choosing **InnoCaptcha**! If you need assistance or have questions, here are the best channels to reach us:

## Questions & Discussions
For general questions, implementation help, or feature suggestions, please use:
* [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/InnoSoft-Company/InnoCaptcha/discussions)
* Our official community platforms.

## Bug Reports
If you encounter a technical bug:
1. Check the [Issues](https://github.com/InnoSoft-Company/InnoCaptcha/issues) page to see if it's already being tracked.
2. If not, open a new issue with clear steps to reproduce the problem and details about your environment (Python version, OS, etc.).

## Professional Services
For custom integrations or inquiries related to **InnoSoft Company**, please contact us through our official business channels.
